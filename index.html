FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Are your electricians licensed?

Yes. We are EMA-licensed electricians, certified by Singapore's Energy Market Authority, and work on HDB, condo, landed and commercial properties.

Will I know the price before you come?

Yes. We check the electrical issue with you first, and the price is quoted and agreed by you before our electricians go down. If we can't assess it accurately beforehand, there may be a small inspection charge, which may be waived if you engage us for the work. See our price guide.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every job comes with a 30-day service warranty, and we only use original (OEM) parts for electrical installations.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. For power trips, power failures and other urgent problems, WhatsApp us at +65 8241 0032 and we'll respond as fast as we can.

When can I reach you?

Our WhatsApp line is open 8AM to 10PM, every day. You can also call +65 3158 6911 (East) or email enquiry@daylightelectrician.com.
