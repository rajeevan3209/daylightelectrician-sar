Tips for hiring an electrician in Singapore

Cheap is not always best. Finding a cheap electrician is easy; finding a cheap licensed electrician is harder.

Hire a licensed electrician

Avoid cheap unlicensed electricians and always hire EMA-licensed electricians. You get standard rates from a reputable company, quality work, and a guarantee in case there are issues after installation.

Divide up the project

Let your electrician assess and list all the electrical works by severity and urgency. If it's over budget, do the critical items first so your home or business stays safe.

Plan your estimate

Whenever you need electrical work done, do a proper estimate for the project that includes both the electrician fees and the cost of materials.

Know when not to DIY

Small jobs that don't involve wiring can be done yourself if you know exactly what you're doing. One error can turn your house into a fire trap, so for anything serious, call an EMA-licensed electrician.
